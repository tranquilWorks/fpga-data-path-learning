## Model and retained deterministic contract

- Inputs: `stageCount` in `1..4`, `enablePeriod` in `1..4` cycles, `selectedCycle` in `1..16`, and `brokenCascade` as logical or numeric `0/1`.
- Trace: 16 rising edges and the fixed pre-edge 4-bit unsigned data codes `[9 2 13 5 14 7 1 12 3 10 6 15 4 11 8 1]`.
- Edge convention: `D[n]` is immediately before edge `n`; `Q[n]` is state immediately after it. A one-stage input/output overlay is not a claim of zero physical latency.
- Priority: synchronous reset is HIGH with enable on cycle 1 and loads zero, proving reset priority. Reset-fill bits are invalid model provenance, not an accepted zero-valued token.
- Enabled first-stage recurrence: `Q1[n]=D[n]`.
- Enabled later-stage recurrence: `Qs[n]=Q(s-1)[n-1]`; every stage uses pre-edge state simultaneously.
- Disabled recurrence: all data state and validity hold their prior values. Enable cadence does not change clock frequency.
- Baseline: one stage, enable period 1, selected cycle 6, no broken cascade. Output is `[0 2 13 5 14 7 1 12 3 10 6 15 4 11 8 1]`; cycles 2 through 16 are valid.
- Depth sweep invariant: first-valid cycles for stages `[1 2 3 4]` are `[2 3 4 5]`; additional enabled-edge delays relative to `Q1` are `[0 1 2 3]`; modeled data storage is `[4 8 12 16]` bits.
- Enable sweep invariant: capture counts for periods `[1 2 3 4]` are `[15 8 5 4]`; hold counts are `[0 7 10 11]`; stage count remains one.
- Combined-lever invariant: with three stages and enable period 3, every data stage and validity bit holds on disabled edges. The cycle-2 token reaches the last stage on the third enabled edge, cycle 8, with output code 2; the output is invalid through cycle 7.
- Broken case: three stages incorrectly reuse each stage's newly updated value on the same edge. At cycle 4, healthy state is `[5 13 2]` and broken state is `[5 5 5]`; cycles 2 through 16 mismatch and cycles 2 through 3 are valid too early. At one stage, this fault is inert.
- Resource contract: every call has 16 cycles, at most four data stages, at most 64 entries in each state/validity trace, and at most 16 modeled data-storage bits. These are model bounds, not measured FPGA utilization.

The Python oracle independently constructs reset/enable events, pre-edge and post-edge states, validity, the correct reference recurrence, and broken updates. Static tests pin the corresponding MATLAB source expressions so an unrelated source implementation cannot pass only through oracle behavior. This still does not execute or translate MATLAB semantics.

### Experiment figures

1. `P03 baseline register`: pre-edge `D` and valid post-edge `Q`, with an explicit invalid reset-fill marker; a second axis shows reset, enable, and output validity.
2. `P03 depth sweep`: last-stage valid codes for one through four stages on the same deterministic input and enable schedule.
3. `P03 enable sweep`: held/captured output traces for periods one through four; a second axis reports captured and held non-reset edge counts.
4. `P03 broken cascade`: healthy versus broken valid output and mismatch markers; a second axis shows the healthy per-stage history.

- Register-depth spinner: integer stages 1 through 4.
- Enable-period spinner: integer period 1 through 4 cycles.
- Selected-cycle spinner: integer edge 1 through 16.
- Broken-cascade checkbox: disabled at one stage; at depth 2 through 4, it violates simultaneous pre-edge sampling.

Every control has an immediate changed callback. The callback captures the P03 model handle, masks invalid state from valid traces, qualifies an inspected invalid fill explicitly, and cleans up only the P03-named UI.

### Metrics and units

- Clock cycle and enable period: cycles.
- Reset, enable, and validity: binary control levels.
- `D`, `Q`, and stage state: unsigned 4-bit codes in `[0..15]`.
- Captures, holds, mismatches, and early-valid occurrences: edge/cycle counts.
- First-valid cycle: cycle index.
- Additional delay: enabled edges relative to `Q1`.
- Modeled data storage: logical data bits only; validity is simulator bookkeeping and no physical resource claim is made.

### Independent system-risk review repair

The review found one medium-severity missing-test risk: the executable MATLAB checks tested depth only with period-1 enable and tested sparse enable only at depth 1. It requires every stage and validity bit to hold on disabled edges and requires the first accepted token to reach the output only on the third enabled edge, cycle 8.
