## Model and retained deterministic contract

- Inputs: resourceUnits is an integer from one through eight modeled multiplier lanes; offeredFrameInterval is an integer from one through eight cycles/frame; selectedFrame is an integer from one through six; brokenFloorSchedule is logical or numeric zero/one. Accepted numeric inputs are normalized after finite, real, scalar, integer, and range validation.
- Workload: each of six deterministic frames contains eight independent products. Input codes are circular shifts of P06's [3,-2,5,1,-4,6,-1,4]; coefficient codes are [2,4,-1,3,2,-2,5,5]. The product path is eight modeled bits with four fractional bits and LSB 1/16.
- Datapath result: an eight-product vector plus an eight-bit validity mask. Reference checksum codes [-9,72,23,-16,69,-6] are diagnostics only; no adder or reduction network is modeled.
- Lane contract: each ideal lane has latency L=2 cycles and initiation interval one product/cycle. For W=8 products and R lanes, healthy frame capacity interval G=ceil(W/R). Frame arrivals are a[j]=1+(j-1)A, starts are s[j]=max(a[j],s[j-1]+G), operation issues are s[j]+floor((k-1)/R), lane identity is 1+mod(k-1,R), and results appear two cycles after issue.
- Rate contract: the raw lanes accept R products/cycle, equivalent to R/8 frame-equivalents of work per cycle. The declared non-preemptive policy leaves partial-group slots idle, so packing utilization 8/(R G) converts the raw work rate to a complete-frame capacity of 1/G frames/cycle. The finite scheduled rate is 1/max(A,G) frames/cycle. A declared 100 MHz teaching assumption converts this normalized rate to Mframes/s; it is not achieved or measured timing.
- Baseline: R=2, A=4, selected frame three. G=4. Arrival and start edges are [1,5,9,13,17,21]; final-product result edges are [6,10,14,18,22,26]. Wait and queue depth are zero, all 48 products are valid, packing is 100 percent, and scheduled rate is 0.25 frame/cycle or 25 Mframes/s under the teaching assumption.
- Resource sweep: R=1 through 8 at A=1 produces capacity intervals [8,4,3,2,2,2,2,1], first-frame arrival-to-final-result edge differences [9,5,4,3,3,3,3,2], maximum finite waits [35,15,10,5,5,5,5,0], and idle final-group slots [0,0,1,0,2,4,6,0]. Products and arrivals remain fixed.
- Demand sweep: A=1 through 8 at R=2 produces scheduled intervals [4,4,4,4,5,6,7,8], maximum waits [15,10,5,0,0,0,0,0], maximum queue depths [4,3,2,0,0,0,0,0], and required ideal lane counts [8,4,3,2,2,2,2,1]. Products, non-preemptive frame capacity, and modeled storage remain fixed.
- Broken case: at R=3 and A=2, floor(8/3)=2 reserves only six slots. Operations seven and eight are invalid/missing from every frame, complete-frame count is zero, and incomplete diagnostic checksums are [-24,47,13,-1,39,-21]. The controller claims 0.5 frame/cycle or 50 Mframes/s while the correct capacity-limited rate is 1/3 frame/cycle. The violated invariant is R*G >= W.
- Fault limit: floor and ceiling agree for exact divisors R=1,2,4,8. For non-divisors R=3,5,6,7, the final uncovered tail is exact and bounded. The fault changes only scheduled coverage and its claims; reference products and healthy schedules remain unchanged.
- Queue contract: queue depth at each clock edge is cumulative arrivals through that edge minus starts through that edge. The checks reconstruct every trace sample independently and pin overloaded maxima.
- Resource contract: at most 48 product records, 50 timeline cycles, 400 lane/timeline slots, 128 modeled product-pipeline bits, and 16 modeled valid-pipeline bits. All loops and arrays are input-bounded.

The Python oracle reconstructs product vectors, ceiling/floor group counts, arrivals, independent start recurrences, issue/result edges, lane identities, validity, exact omitted tails, completion claims, event-derived queue traces, collision maps, diagnostic sums, and resource bounds. It is not generated from or executed through model.m. Static tests pin corresponding MATLAB formulas, guards, normalization, labels, model/presentation separation, source-level absence of opaque/toolbox/stateful/external/asynchronous operations, and forward-compatible lifecycle facts. These checks still do not execute MATLAB semantics.
