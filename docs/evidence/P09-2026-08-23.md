## Model and deterministic contract

- Inputs: `sourceGapCycles` is an integer `0..3`; `readyStallCycles` is an integer `0..6`; `brokenAdvanceWithoutReady` is logical or numeric zero/one. Accepted integer classes normalize to double/logical after finite, real, scalar, integer, and range validation.
- Sampling convention: `valid`, `data`, and `ready` are observed immediately before rising edge `n`; `transfer[n] = valid[n] && ready[n]` at that edge.
- Ownership and hold rule: the producer owns `valid` and `data`; the consumer owns `ready`. After a cycle with `valid=1` and `ready=0`, a healthy producer keeps `valid`, payload code, and token identity unchanged for the next cycle. A transfer permits next-cycle state to change.
- Fixed teaching data: eight unsigned 12-bit P08 phase-code labels `[0, 411, 822, 1233, 1644, 2055, 2466, 2877]`; one 35-cycle record; ready-stall start at cycle 5.
- Baseline: one source-gap cycle and ready low on cycles 5 through 7. Transfers occur at `[1, 3, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18]`. Token 822 is offered unchanged on cycles 5 through 8 and waits three cycles. All eight tokens arrive once and in order. Through completion there are eight transfers, three stalled-valid cycles, seven source bubbles, mean wait `3/8` cycles/token, maximum wait three cycles, and modeled rate `8/18` transfers/cycle.
- Truth partition: each cycle is exactly one of neither asserted, ready without valid, stalled valid without ready, or transfer. Ready alone does not invent data, and valid alone does not consume it.
- Source-gap sweep: with ready always high, gaps `0..3` yield completion cycles `[8, 15, 22, 29]`, source-bubble counts `[0, 7, 14, 21]`, and modeled completion-window rates `8 ./ [8, 15, 22, 29]`. Ready, payload identity, order, accepted count, hold behavior, and allocation remain fixed.
- Ready-stall sweep: with zero source gaps, ready-low durations `0..6` yield completion cycles `8..14`; stalled-valid count and maximum token wait each equal the selected duration. Source supply, payload identity, accepted order/count, and allocation remain fixed.
- Combined overlap boundary: with a two-cycle source gap, ready low on cycles 5-6 occurs wholly while `valid=0`, so transfers remain `[1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22]` with no stalled-valid cycle or added wait. Extending ready low through cycle 7 overlaps only token 822's offer, so that token waits one cycle, transfers move to `[1, 4, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23]`, and the 14 source bubbles remain unchanged. Consumer unavailability delays the stream only where it overlaps a valid offer.
- Limits: continuous valid with always-ready service transfers once per cycle and finishes at cycle 8; early ready during a source bubble transfers nothing; the longest stall holds token 1644 through cycles 5 to 11 and completes at cycle 14; the maximum healthy gap/stall combination completes exactly on the final record edge, cycle 35.
- Broken case: with zero source gaps and a three-cycle ready stall, the producer incorrectly advances after a valid offer without requiring ready. It drops token indices 5 through 7, payloads `[1644, 2055, 2466]`, on cycles 5 through 7. The consumer receives indices `[1, 2, 3, 4, 8]`, and hold violations appear on cycles 6 through 8. The complete healthy reference remains unchanged. With no stalled-valid cycle, the fault is behaviorally inert.
- Resource contract: exactly 35 trace values and eight token slots per path, with a declared modeled 12-bit payload hold width. All loops and arrays are bounded by validated controls or fixed constants. These are teaching-model allocation counts, not registers, memory primitives, area, power, throughput, or timing evidence.
- Liveness boundary: every exposed healthy schedule returns ready within at most six cycles and completes within the fixed record. The transfer and stable-hold safety rules do not by themselves guarantee progress if a consumer never reasserts ready; indefinite stall, timeout policy, and system-level deadlock are outside this model.

`tests/test_p09_module.py` implements the source, consumer schedule, transfer accounting, stable-hold detector, loss detector, completion metrics, and both fault paths independently rather than evaluating or parsing MATLAB results. It executes the exact baseline, the truth partition, both complete sweeps, the wholly hidden and partially overlapping ready-low/source-bubble boundaries, key limits, the exact broken symptom, all 56 healthy/fault control combinations, malformed-input/recovery behavior, call isolation, and fixed resource bounds. Static tests pin the corresponding MATLAB structure and expressions and prohibit presentation in the model, random/external/stateful/asynchronous behavior, and unbounded `while` loops. These checks do not execute MATLAB semantics.
