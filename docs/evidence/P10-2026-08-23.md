## Governing model and retained simulated outputs

Signals are pre-edge observations. The FIFO is registered and non-fall-through:

    dequeue[n] = outputValid[n] && consumerReady[n]
    sourceReady[n] = (q[n] < D) || dequeue[n]
    enqueue[n] = sourceValid[n] && sourceReady[n]
    q[n+1] = q[n] + enqueue[n] - dequeue[n]

The `dequeue` term permits a full FIFO to remove and replace one token on the same edge. It does not permit fall-through: token 1 enqueues on cycle 1 and first dequeues on cycle 2.

- Combined boundary: at depth four, three ready-low cycles fill the three free slots without pausing the source, complete on cycle 16, and leave the selected broken mode behaviorally inert.
