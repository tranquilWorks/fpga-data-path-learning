## Governing model and retained simulated outputs

The FIFO is empty initially and registered, not fall-through. On edge `n`, stored words may depart
before admission and their slots may be replaced on that same edge, but a newly arriving word cannot
depart until a later edge:

    departed[n] = min(service[n], qBefore[n])
    qAfter[n] = qBefore[n] - departed[n] + arrivals[n]
    Drequired = max(qAfter[n])

For a constant integer arrival rate `A` over `B` cycles and constant service capacity `S`, this
contract reduces to:

    Drequired = A + (B - 1) * max(A - S, 0)

The first term is the registered staging batch. It remains even when service matches or exceeds the
arrival rate. Finite admission is simulated only after the lossless reference has established the
required depth:

    free[n] = D - (qBefore[n] - departed[n])
    admitted[n] = min(arrivals[n], free[n])
    notAccepted[n] = arrivals[n] - admitted[n]

`notAccepted` means the upstream must hold and retry under valid/ready flow control. It is a loss
count only if that source cannot or does not obey backpressure.

### Exact baseline

Inputs and bounds: burst duration 8 cycles; aggregate arrival rate 3 words/cycle; guaranteed stored
word service 1 word/cycle; configured registered depth 17 words; fixed 40-cycle observation; empty
initial FIFO.

- The burst offers 24 words on cycles 1–8.
- Occupancy after those eight edges is `[3,5,7,9,11,13,15,17]` words.
- Peak occupancy is 17 words on cycle 8; the exact installed depth therefore has zero margin.
- Service removes one stored word on cycles 2–25, and the lossless reference drains on cycle 25.
- All 24 words are admitted and depart; no word requires upstream hold/retry.
- Per-edge unbounded and finite recurrence residuals, finite offer residuals, and both final
  conservation residuals are exactly zero.
- Cumulative arrivals minus cumulative departures equals registered occupancy at every edge.

### Independent sweep 1 — burst duration

Arrival stays 3 words/cycle, service stays 1 word/cycle, selected capacity stays 32 words, and the
fault remains off. Burst duration sweeps `[1,2,4,6,8]` cycles.

- Offered words: `[3,6,12,18,24]`.
- Required depths: `[3,5,9,13,17]` words.
- Drain-completion cycles: `[4,7,13,19,25]`.
- Every case admits all offered words and preserves the fixed rate assumptions.

### Independent sweep 2 — service rate

Burst duration stays eight cycles, arrival stays 3 words/cycle, selected capacity stays 32 words,
and the fault remains off. Service sweeps `0:4` words/cycle.

- Required depths: `[24,17,10,3,3]` words.
- Drain-completion cycles: `[NaN,25,13,9,9]`; `NaN` is the explicit bounded no-drain result for
  zero service, not an execution timeout.
- The matched and faster cases still require three registered staging words and drain on cycle 9.
- Every case admits the complete fixed 24-word burst.

### Limits and deliberately broken case

- Zero service retains all 24 words after the burst, performs no departure, returns after the fixed
  40-cycle trace, and reports no completed drain.
- Matched service (`A=S=3`) and faster service (`A=3`, `S=4`) both require three words because the
  registered FIFO cannot serve the new first-edge arrivals immediately. Same-edge departure and
  replacement then sustain the offered rate.
- The deliberately broken estimate uses fall-through arithmetic,
  `B*max(A-S,0) = 16` words, for the registered baseline. Its finite path refuses exactly one word
  on cycle 8, admits and drains 23 words, and exposes the precise one-word sizing error without
  changing the lossless reference.
- With zero service the broken and registered estimates both equal the full 24-word burst, so fault
  selection is intentionally inert.
- For positive service throughout the complete grid, the broken estimate always omits the
  registered staging requirement and produces at least one not-accepted word. With zero service it
  remains inert.
- All 160 burst/arrival/service combinations (`8×4×5`) execute healthy and broken paths. Exact-depth
  healthy paths conserve all words. Positive-service healthy paths drain by cycle 33; zero-service
  paths return a bounded no-drain result. Repeated calls are exactly deterministic and the broken
  path never mutates the reference.
